Meet Shihan V.Anitha

I’m Anitha – a dedicated karate practitioner, women’s self-defense coach, and personal safety strategist. My mission is to make every woman feel safe, strong, and in control of her life.

Karate gave me more than discipline and technique—it taught me awareness, focus, and inner strength. I teach self-defense to ensure women are not just told to be careful, but taught how to be powerful.

  • 5th Dan Black Belt (Since 2004) & National Coach
  • 5000+ Students Trained in Personal Safety
  • First Aid CPR AED Certified (American Heart Association)
